Aravalli Cables Bags Export Order for US Data Center

JAIPUR — Aravalli Cables Limited today announced the following.

Aravalli Cables has received an export order worth Rs 371 crore for high-voltage cables for a data center project in the United States.

Exports accounted for 32% of revenue in the first half of fiscal 2027.

We continue to monitor supply chain conditions, including lead time trends for transformers and switchgear.
