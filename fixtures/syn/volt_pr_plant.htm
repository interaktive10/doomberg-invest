Voltara to Build Transformer Factory in Texas

HOUSTON — Voltara Grid Systems, Inc. today announced the following.

Voltara will build a 600,000 square foot transformer factory in Texas to expand capacity for grid and data center customers.

We continue to monitor supply chain conditions, including lead time trends for transformers and switchgear.

Current lead times for medium-voltage switchgear are 41 weeks.

The Board authorized a new share repurchase program of up to $2.0 billion.
