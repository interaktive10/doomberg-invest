Keystone Power & Light Co.

PART I

Item 1. Business

We are a regulated electric utility serving customers across three states.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our operations depend on the reliable delivery of power to our customers, and extreme weather events could disrupt our transmission and distribution systems and increase our costs significantly.

We depend on third-party suppliers for large power transformers and switchgear, and supply shortages could delay the interconnection of new customer loads and increase our capital expenditures.

Changes in federal and state tax law could affect our effective tax rate and cash flows in ways that are difficult to predict today.

Regulatory decisions on our rate cases determine the returns we may earn on our investments, and adverse decisions could reduce our earnings and cash flows.

Cyber attacks on our systems or those of our vendors could disrupt operations and expose us to liability, regulatory penalties and reputational harm.

None.
