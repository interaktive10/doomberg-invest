Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

We continue to monitor supply chain conditions, including lead time trends for transformers and switchgear.

Total backlog reached $13.2 billion at quarter end, a record for the company.

Backlog from data center customers was $4.1 billion, up from the prior year.

Lead times for large power transformers extended to 147 weeks during the quarter.

Our stock price rose 23% during the quarter, and two analysts upgraded their ratings to buy.

Our largest customer, a hyperscale data center operator, accounted for 26% of revenue in the quarter.

We entered into a multi-year agreement to supply power transformers and switchgear to Nimbus Compute for its AI data center campuses.
