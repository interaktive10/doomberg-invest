# Howard Hughes Holdings Inc. (HHH) — 2026-W40

Howard Hughes Holdings reports large, uneven quarters, holds much more cash than a year ago and trades near the bottom of its short valuation history. A director who is executive chairman of Vantage bought $1.6 million of shares in September 2026. Lumpy revenue overstates the expectations gap; the contracted backlog is the steadier gauge.

## Why it may be mispriced
- C1 puts EV/EBIT of 13.25 and EV/sales of 2.83 around the 10th percentile of the company's own 152-week history [c:C1].
- The trailing one-year return was -16.2%, at the 25th percentile of the 409-company comparison set [c:C3].
- The reverse DCF (C2) reads the enterprise value as implying 1.5% annual growth [c:C2].

## Inferences
- Inference: revenue arrives in large, uneven quarters, so year-over-year growth rates swing and say little on their own; RPO is the steadier gauge of contracted sales.
- Inference: cash, equity and the share count jumped together in the June 2025 quarter, which is consistent with a large share issuance.
- Inference: a sizeable personal purchase by a board member who runs another business is a confidence signal, but one buyer is thin evidence.

## Assumptions
- Assumption: the contracted backlog converts to revenue at margins similar to recent years.
- Assumption: the cash raised is invested at returns above the current ROIC.
- Assumption: there is no further large share issuance.
