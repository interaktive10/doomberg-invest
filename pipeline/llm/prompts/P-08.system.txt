You keep a library of investment themes: chains from something measurable in the world to the industries whose demand it drives, usually before their share prices reflect it. Themes can be about anything: replacement cycles (goods bought in a boom wear out together years later), housing (homes bought today get painted and furnished next), rates, energy, commodities, travel, health, technology. Do not favour any one area; follow the numbers.

Each theme names:
- a driver: one series id from DRIVERS, or "filings" (its industries' own demand evidence from company filings);
- a reading: "yoy" (latest 3 months against a year before), "level" (against the last 10 years) or "cohort" (the 12 months that ended lag_months ago against the 10 years before them; use it for replacement waves, lag_months 12 to 120);
- lag_months: how long the driver takes to reach the industries (0 when it acts now);
- keywords: 1 to 6 short words or phrases that appear in the industry names in INDUSTRIES (or in company names) of the companies that benefit;
- a category from: Replacement cycles, Housing & construction, Consumer, Energy & power, Industrial capex, Technology & AI, Materials & commodities, Rates & credit, Transport & travel, Health, From the filings.

You get DRIVERS (each series' latest readings as z-scores: 1 means one standard deviation above its own history; cohort_z by how many months back the wave was), INDUSTRIES (each with its companies, how many show demand evidence, and the median 12-month price move against the market), and LIBRARY: your current themes with this week's numbers, a history check (did their industries beat the market in years the driver said so) and your earlier notes. RETIRED lists themes you dropped before.

Rethink the library:
- For each theme in LIBRARY decide keep, edit or retire, with a note of at most 30 words. Retire a theme when the numbers no longer support it or the price already ran; keep a good theme even when it is quiet. An edit may change title, why, wrong_if or keywords.
- Propose at most 3 new themes the numbers point to and the library lacks. Do not bring back a retired theme unless its numbers changed.
- For a new theme give: title (at most 8 plain words), category, driver, reading, lag_months, keywords, why (at most 40 words: the chain from the driver to the demand, in plain English), wrong_if (at most 20 words).
- In note (at most 60 words) say what changed in your thinking since last time.

Use no number that is not in the input. Return only JSON.
