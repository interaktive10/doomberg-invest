19. **§4.10.3/§4.10.5 "the Newey-West t-stat (lags = horizon)" over-rejects at the brief's lags** (M8 review R1-F2).
    With lags as long as a third of the period and events clustered in a few months, the Newey-West variance of a mean
    excess is noisy and often far below the plain one. On iid noise over an 84-month period (60 events, 2,000 draws),
    P(|t| >= 2) is 9.4% / 15.3% / 18.6% at lags 12 / 24 / 36 against 4-5% for a floored or plain t; on F-LAB (seeds
    1-16) the noise signal crosses |t| = 2 in 11 of 32 cells unfloored, 2 of 32 floored. §4.10.5 would then promote or
    demote weights on chance. Built: t_nw uses max(NW SE, plain SE), so it is never larger than the plain t; the
    report says so; lab_results.notes keeps the unfloored t (`t_nw_unfloored=`) next to the lower bound. The floor only
    moves §4.10.5 outcomes toward "insignificant" (weight 1.0; legacy 0). **Decision needed (Manzoor):** keep the floor,
    or use the unfloored NW t as written.

An order book that covers 6% of revenue can't lead revenue, and the same jump feeds C2's evidence growth (40% weight on g(ob), clipped at 0.6), which gave AMR a g_e of 16.1% against a 3-year revenue CAGR of about −16%. Without S1, AMR has one family (behavior) and is not a candidate. **Proposal:** S1 and the g(ob) term of C2's evidence growth apply only when ob / TTM revenue ≥ 0.25 (or S1's strength is scaled by min(1, coverage / 0.5)). **Decision needed (Manzoor):** §4.6 and §4.7.

43. **C2 reads backwards when growth lowers the reverse DCF's value.** Each $1 of extra revenue costs 1/S2C of capital and earns m(1 − t) a year. The present value therefore rises with g only when m(1 − t) × S2C is above the discount rate (9% US, 12% IN). Below that, PV falls as g rises: a low enterprise value gives a *higher* g*, so C2 = g_e − g* has the wrong sign. With S2C at its 0.5 floor, that is any after-tax margin under 18% (US); at the 1.5 default, under 6%. In 2026-W40:
    - **AMR** (0.082 × 0.5 = 0.041): C2 scored 95.6 and lifted its Gap to 60.6. Without C2 its Gap is 43.0, which makes it priced-in, not a candidate.
    - **EMBC** (0.154 × 0.5 = 0.077): C2 scored 13.2 and pulled its Gap down to 65.9. Without C2 its Gap is 92.3.
    - **Matador and Howard Hughes** are in the normal regime (0.18 and 0.31).
    - **Howard Hughes, a separate issue:** its g_e hits the 0.40 cap because the latest quarter's revenue was up 330% YoY on lumpy land and condo sales.

    **Proposal:** C2 is null when m(1 − t) × S2C ≤ the discount rate, because the model can't read an expectation from the price there. g_e uses TTM revenue YoY rather than the latest quarter's. **Decision needed (Manzoor):** §4.7 and config/context.yaml are protected.

    **Combined effect on 2026-W40, if 41–43 were applied:** candidates CELH, EMBC, HHH and MTDR. AMR and PFE drop out. Other companies may enter, because a C2 that read backwards also pulled some Gap scores below 50; only a re-score shows which. The thesis cards for AMR and PFE (data/thesis_cards/2026-W40 on branch `thesis-cards-2026-w40`) already say this in their inferences.
